About

One marketer, three brands, and the strategy work most titles don't cover.

I've been the digital marketing executive at Kachhua Solutions since January 2025, running fotonVR, RadiumXR, and MedionXR; three XR education and training brands, at the same time, each with its own audience, market, and ad strategy.

The job is wider than the title. I build websites from scratch on code and no-code tools, write and run the ad campaigns, plan SEO/AEO/GEO content, manage the LinkedIn presence for three company leaders, and sit in on core planning for where the company goes next, quarterly strategy, revenue plans, and how money moves through the business.

Before this, I generated leads for TopVocation, sold vehicles at a Maruti Suzuki dealership, and ran an esports team for two years, recruiting players, building rosters, and reading performance data to make the next call. Different rooms, same instinct: find what's working, cut what isn't, do it again.

I'm finishing a B.Tech in Computer Engineering alongside all of this, which is part of why I end up in the technical side of marketing, SEO infrastructure, schema, site builds, as much as the creative side.
